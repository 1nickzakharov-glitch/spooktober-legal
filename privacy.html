Spooktober Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 October 2026 · Applies to the Spooktober app for iOS and Android

Spooktober ("the app") is a Halloween countdown with widgets, reminders and small games. This policy explains what happens to your information. The short version: the app does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

1. Information we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting service, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. The app does not contain trackers and does not access your contacts, photos, location, microphone, camera or advertising identifier.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following only on your own device, in the device's local app storage: your selected theme and widget style, your reminder settings, your game progress and high scores, your pumpkin collection (the pumpkins you carved), your sound and vibration preferences, and the date of your last visit (used for the daily streak). This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

3. Notifications

If you switch reminders on, the app asks your operating system for permission to show notifications and schedules them locally on your device. No server is involved. You can switch reminders off in the app or in your system settings at any time.

4. Sharing

When you tap Share, the app opens the standard share sheet of your operating system with text or an image you chose to share. What happens next depends on the app you pick (for example, a messenger). We do not receive that content.

5. Network access

The app works fully offline. Its fonts, images, sounds and models are bundled inside the app, and it makes no network requests of its own.

6. Purchases

If optional purchases are offered in a future version, they will be processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We will not receive your payment details. We will update this policy before such a change.

7. Children

The app is suitable for a general audience and contains only mild, cartoon-style Halloween themes. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (US COPPA) or under 16 (EU GDPR). It contains no ads and no links that lead out of the app.

8. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. Everything the app stores is on your device, and you can remove it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. This applies to users everywhere, including the EU/EEA, the UK and California.

9. Security

Data stays inside the app's private storage on your device and is protected by your operating system's own security.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address and the effective date above will be updated. Material changes will also be announced in the app.
